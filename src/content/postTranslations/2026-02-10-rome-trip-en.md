## 📅 Suggested 4-Day, 3-Night Rome Itinerary (Based Near Zalib)

A 4-day, 3-night itinerary in Rome after the trip to Germany. The accommodation is just north of **Trastevere**, near **Zalib (Via della Penitenza)**, so the location is excellent! It is the ideal base for getting to both the Vatican and the city center.

### 🏠 Advantages of the Location
- Near **Zalib**, Trastevere's hip vibe and quiet coexist.
- Just a 15-minute walk to **St. Peter's Square (Vatican)**!
- Just a 10-minute walk to **Castel Sant'Angelo**!
- Cross a bridge and you are in the city center, including Piazza Navona.

### 🗺️ Recommended Route

**Day 1: Arrive in Rome and a Night in Trastevere**
- Check in and unpack lightly
- **Gianicolo (Janiculum Hill)**: It is right behind the accommodation, and at sunset you can see the whole city of Rome. (Recommended if you still have energy)
- **Santa Maria in Trastevere**: Stroll around Trastevere's central square.
- Dinner: pasta at a nearby favorite such as `Tonnarello` or `Nannarella`.

**Day 2: Vatican and Riverside Stroll (Walking Day)**
- The accommodation is close, so walk to **St. Peter's Square** early in the morning.
- **Vatican Museums** (morning reservation recommended).
- In the afternoon, take photos on the bridge in front of **Castel Sant'Angelo**.
- In the evening, enjoy the atmosphere over a beer or glass of wine at **Zalib**, right next to the accommodation.

**Day 3: The "Real" Roman Ruins Tour**
- Cross a bridge (such as Ponte Sisto) into the city center.
- **Pantheon** -> **Trevi Fountain** -> **Spanish Steps** (shopping district).
- **Colosseum** and **Roman Forum**: majestic even from the outside.
- It is a day of lots of walking, so comfortable shoes are a must!

**Day 4: A Reluctant Farewell**
- Espresso or cappuccino at a nearby cafe such as **Caffè Lungara 1940**.
- Head to the airport (you can take the train from Trastevere station to FCO).

Have a great trip! 🇮🇹🍕🍷
