- **Breakfast:** 06:00-10:00 on weekdays, 06:00-10:30 on weekends. On day one, if immigration finishes early, eat pho at Pho Ha and head to the massage. If time is short, skip the restaurant stop and prepare a simple breakfast. Hotel breakfast is not assumed on the check-in day, and whether later breakfasts are included depends on the booking terms.
- **Swimming pool:** 3rd floor, daily **06:00-22:00**. About an hour on the afternoon of 10/10 is recommended. [Official pool information](https://www.novotel-saigon-centre.com/spa-fitness/swimming-pool/)
- **OnTop Bar:** The hotel's rooftop on the 20th floor. If you do not feel like traveling far at night, enjoy the night view from the hotel. Drinks are ordered separately, and it is a different facility from the Premier Lounge on the 19th floor.
- **Luggage storage on day one:** Leave luggage at the front desk in the morning, join the tour, and check in in the evening. It is advisable to tell the hotel in advance about the morning luggage storage and the evening check-in. Regular check-in starts at 14:00, and early-morning room use is not included in the itinerary.

## Sat 10/10 · Russian Market, Central Post Office, Notre-Dame and the Riverside Night View

Considering the fatigue from the full-day tour the day before, the day is split into **a morning city stroll → midday hotel rest → evening by the river**. The times below are a recommended layout and not booked activities.

The morning stops chosen by the traveler are **[Russian Market](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vzii2828Bc7UhtL78) → Central Post Office → Notre-Dame Cathedral**. The market's address confirmed on the map is **125 Hai Bà Trưng**, close to the Central Post Office. Walk down from Novotel to see all three together, and leave the shopping at the hotel after lunch to rest.

| Time | Activity | Notes |

If shopping at the Russian Market runs long, skip the bookstreet and cafe and keep the post office and cathedral. The map pins are building reference points for the [Central Post Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saigon_Central_Post_Office) and [Notre-Dame Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_Cathedral_Basilica_of_Saigon); follow the actual pedestrian entrances and passable streets.

The [Saigon Water Bus official FAQ](https://saigonwaterbus.com/cau-hoi-thuong-gap) lists **15,000 dong one way** and says round trips require booking the outbound and return sailings separately. The last sailing may not return to the departure point, so choose a sailing that allows a round trip and arrive at the pier 15 minutes before boarding. The 16:00 in the table above is not the boat's departure time but the recommended time to start moving.

If it rains, swap the riverside plan for a cafe, indoor shopping or a massage. If a show appeals more, the [A O Show](https://www.luneproduction.com/ao-show) is an evening alternative, but performances and seats for 10/10 have not been checked yet.
